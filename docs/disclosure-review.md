# Disclosure implementation and outstanding legal review

Reviewed September 18, 2026. This is an implementation record and a list for qualified counsel, not a legal opinion or certification of compliance.

The entry screen requires an unchecked acknowledgment on every page load, including browser back/forward restoration. The research is initially hidden and inert; application modules load only after acknowledgment. The notice remains available inside the site. No identifying acknowledgment record or signed agreement is collected. This is a client-side presentation gate, not access control: public static files remain directly accessible. No liability release, indemnity, mandatory arbitration or waiver of investor rights was added.

The disclosure addresses general education, lack of personalized advice, client-relationship limits, digital-asset risks, third-party data limitations, subjective scores, hypothetical scenarios and preservation of legal duties. Forecast headings were relabeled as hypothetical scenarios, with more prominent assumptions and loss/cost limitations. Relabeling and a disclaimer do not establish that forecasts are lawful advertisements.

## Material items still requiring review

- Confirm the operator's actual Florida investment-adviser representative registration, advisory firm, SEC versus state registration, supervisory policies, outside-business approval, and whether this site constitutes adviser advertising. Passing Series 65 alone does not resolve these questions. No firm approval or regulatory status was asserted on the site.
- Evaluate public numerical forecasts before continued use. Where the SEC marketing rule applies, unrestricted website hypothetical performance can require controls beyond a checkbox and warnings. Consider removing public forecasts pending approval. Do not assume the interactive-analysis-tool exception applies merely because users can select a coin.
- Obtain and disclose actual material interests: holdings, trading, ownership, compensation, affiliate/referral arrangements, issuer payments and project/developer relationships, including Tari-related work. Generic language is not a substitute for disclosing known conflicts. No holdings or absence of compensation was invented. The disclosure describes only the site's verifiable Tari infrastructure use.
- Counsel should assess current Florida Chapter 517 and applicable 69W rules, federal adviser anti-fraud and marketing provisions, securities paid-promotion requirements if applicable, firm recordkeeping and supervision, and other jurisdictions served. State rule text was not successfully retrieved in this review; this is not an exhaustive all-laws analysis.
- Retain approved site versions, underlying sources, substantiation, performance calculations, disclosures and supervisory sign-offs under the firm's applicable recordkeeping schedule. Git history alone is not a determination of compliant records retention.

## Authorities consulted

- Florida Legislature, [section 517.301](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0517/Sections/0517.301.html): fraud and material misstatements or omissions; paid securities publicity and registration/endorsement representations. Applicability depends on the activity and facts.
- SEC, [Investment Adviser Marketing guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing): scope for SEC-registered or required-to-register advisers, balanced and substantiated advertising, hypothetical-performance conditions and records.
- SEC, [2024 public-website hypothetical-performance enforcement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-46): illustrates why public forecasts need separate compliance review.
- SEC, [Commission Interpretation Regarding Standard of Conduct for Investment Advisers](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2019/ia-5248.pdf): fiduciary obligations and conflicts; disclosures cannot erase nonwaivable duties.
- [Investment Advisers Act section 215](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/80b-15): statutory limitations on waivers.

Have the firm's compliance officer and Florida securities counsel review the actual website, business model and interests. A checkbox cannot change the legal character of substantive investment advice or cure misleading content.
